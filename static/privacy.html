AuraCare AI - Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

AuraCare AI ("we", "our", or "us") operates the AuraCare AI Hospital Management SaaS and WhatsApp booking assistant. We are committed to protecting your personal data and your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to our patients and users:

  • Personal Identification: Name, phone number, data of birth, gender, and contact details.

We use the collected information for clinical triage, booking appointments, sending prescriptions, coordinating ambulance/emergency dispatches, and hospital administration.

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.
